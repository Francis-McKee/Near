# Near
A non-addictive social network designed to foster meaningful, real-life connections without the engagement mechanics that keep people scrolling.

## Why Near?

Social media should help us maintain meaningful relationships, not compete for our attention. Near is built around the idea that staying connected with people you know shouldn't require endless scrolling, popularity metrics, or pressure to check in every day.

Near prioritizes intentional use, mutual relationships, and a feed that lets you catch up and move on with your day.

## Non-addictive Features

1. Healthy social connections
- Mutual friendships: Both people must accept a friend request.
- Real-life connections: Designed for connecting with people users already know.
- No follower counts: Relationships are mutual rather than one-sided.
- No like counts: Avoids popularity contests and social comparison.
- No public popularity metrics: No visible metrics designed to measure a user's social worth.
- Friends-only posts: Only accepted friends can view a user's posts.

2. A finite, intentional feed
- No infinite scrolling: The feed has a clear stopping point.
- Chronological feed: Posts are ordered by time rather than engagement.
- No algorithmic recommendations: No algorithm designed to maximize engagement.
- No discovery or interest page: No endless browsing of strangers or recommended content.
- Clear caught-up state: Users know when they've seen all available posts from their friends.

3. No compulsive engagement mechanics
- No notifications: No push notifications or in-app notifications designed to pull users back into the app.
- No streaks: No daily-use streaks, attendance rewards, or pressure to maintain consecutive days of activity.
- No engagement-driven ranking: Posts aren't promoted based on likes, comments, or other engagement metrics.
- No artificial urgency: No countdowns or expiring engagement incentives designed to create fear of missing out.

4. No advertising incentives
- No advertisements.
- No sponsored content.
- No monetization model based on maximizing screen time or engagement.

5. Privacy and user control
- Mutual consent for friendships.
- Restricted post visibility: Posts are available only to accepted friends.
- Account deletion: Users can leave the platform and request deletion of their data.

## Planned Features

Near is under active development. Core functionality includes authentication, profiles, text posts, a chronological friends-only feed, and mutual friendships through invitation links.

Planned improvements include:
- Purchase and configure a custom domain.
- Allow users to include images in posts.
